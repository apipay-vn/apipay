# Custom Domains

Use your own domain (e.g., `pay.yourdomain.com`) instead of ApiPay's default domain for your payment pages. This gives your customers a branded experience while you retain full control over your domain.

## Overview

When you create a payment request, ApiPay generates a payment page URL. By default, it uses ApiPay's domain. With Custom Domains, you can replace this with your own subdomain, providing a seamless and professional experience for your customers.

**Key benefits:**
- Branded payment URLs (`pay.yourdomain.com`) instead of default ApiPay URLs
- Customers see your domain, increasing trust
- Works alongside existing payment request features

---

## Prerequisites

Before setting up a custom domain, make sure you have:

- A subdomain you control (e.g., `pay.yourdomain.com`, `checkout.yourdomain.com`)
- Access to your DNS provider (e.g., GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap)
- An ApiPay account with at least one connected bank account

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

### Step 1: Add your domain in the Dashboard

1. Log in to the [ApiPay Dashboard](https://dashboard.apipay.vn)
2. Navigate to **Integrations → Custom Domains**
3. Enter your subdomain (e.g., `pay.yourdomain.com`) in the input field
4. Click **Add**

The system generates a unique verification token and saves your domain with a **Pending** status.

### Step 2: Configure DNS Records

You need to add two DNS records with your domain provider.

#### DNS Record 1: TXT Record (Domain Verification)

This proves to ApiPay that you own the domain.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | TXT |
| Name / Host | `pay` (for `pay.yourdomain.com`) or `@` (for apex domain `yourdomain.com`) |
| Value | `apipay-verification=<TOKEN>` |

To get the token:
1. Go to **Integrations → Custom Domains** in the Dashboard
2. Find your domain in the table
3. Click the **...** menu on the right side of the row
4. Click **Copy Token**

Paste the full token (including `apipay-verification=`) as the TXT record value.

#### DNS Record 2: A Record (Traffic Routing)

This routes traffic from your domain to ApiPay's server.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | A |
| Name / Host | `pay` (for subdomain) or `@` (for apex domain) |
| Value | `<SERVER_IP>` |

Click **Copy IP** on the DNS instructions card in the Dashboard to copy the server IP.

> **Important:** For apex domains (e.g., `yourdomain.com`), adding an A record may interfere with your email service (MX records). If you use this domain for email, we recommend using a subdomain instead (e.g., `pay.yourdomain.com`).

### Step 3: Wait for DNS Propagation

DNS changes typically take effect within a few minutes but can take up to 48 hours in rare cases. Most DNS providers propagate within 5–15 minutes.

### Step 4: Verify your domain

You have two options:

**Option A — Manual Verification:**

1. Go to **Integrations → Custom Domains** in the Dashboard
2. Find your domain in the table (status should be **Pending**)
3. Click the **...** menu on the right side of the row
4. Click **Verify**

ApiPay immediately checks your TXT DNS record. If the token is found, the domain status changes to **Verified**.

**Option B — Automatic Verification:**

ApiPay automatically checks TXT records for unverified domains every 15 minutes. If the record is found, the domain is verified automatically and you'll receive a success email.

### Step 5: Use your custom domain

Once your domain shows a green **Verified** badge in the Dashboard:

1. Create a new payment request (or edit an existing one)
2. In the payment request form, select your custom domain from the **Custom Domain** dropdown
3. When you share the payment link with your customer, it will use your domain instead of ApiPay's default domain

## DNS Propagation & Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Verification fails right after adding TXT record | Wait 5–15 minutes for DNS to propagate, then try again |
| Manual Verify button isn't available | Make sure the domain status is still **Pending** and a token exists |
| Auto-verify hasn't triggered after 15 minutes | Use the **Verify** button in the Dashboard for immediate verification |
| Domain status changed to inactive | The domain was unverified for more than 24 hours. Delete it and re-add it following the steps above |
| Apex domain breaks email delivery | Switch to a subdomain (e.g., `pay.yourdomain.com`) instead of using the apex domain |
| TXT record not found | Double-check the record name and value. The token must match exactly, including `apipay-verification=` |

---

## Auto-Expiration

Domains that remain unverified for more than **24 hours** are automatically marked as **inactive** by ApiPay. You will receive an email notification with instructions.

To re-activate:
1. Delete the inactive domain from the Dashboard
2. Re-add it following the [Step-by-Step Setup](#step-by-step-setup) process above
