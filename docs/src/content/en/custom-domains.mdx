# Custom Domains

Use your own domain (for example `pay.yourdomain.com`) for the payment page instead of ApiPay's default domain, so customers see your brand.

## What you need

- A domain you control. A subdomain such as `pay.yourdomain.com` or `checkout.yourdomain.com` is recommended.
- Access to edit DNS at your domain provider (for example Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap).
- At least one connected bank.

---

## Steps

### Step 1: Add the domain

1. Sign in at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn) and open **Integrations → Custom Domains**.
2. Enter your domain and click **Add**.

The domain appears in the list as **Pending**.

### Step 2: Add DNS records

Add these two records at your domain provider. The example uses `pay.yourdomain.com`; for a root domain, enter `@` as the Name / Host.

| Type | Name / Host | Value |
|------|-------------|-------|
| TXT | `pay` | `apipay-verification=<TOKEN>` |
| A | `pay` | ApiPay server IP address |

- **Token**: in the domain list, open the **...** menu and choose **Copy Token**. Paste all of it, including `apipay-verification=`.
- **IP address**: copy it from the DNS instructions card on the Custom Domains page.

### Step 3: Verify

DNS usually updates within minutes, sometimes longer (up to 48 hours).

- To check now: open the domain's **...** menu and choose **Verify**.
- Or wait: ApiPay checks periodically and emails you once the domain is verified.

When verification succeeds, the domain shows **Verified**.

A domain not verified within 24 hours becomes inactive and you get an email with instructions. Delete it and add it again from step 1.

### Step 4: Use your domain

When you create a payment request, open **Advanced Settings** and pick your domain. The payment link will use it.

Using the API? Send `customDomainId` (copy it with **Copy Domain ID** in the **...** menu). See the [Payment Requests API](/en/api/payment-requests).

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Verification fails right after adding records | Wait a few minutes for DNS to update, then click **Verify** again. |
| TXT record not found | Double-check the record name and value. The token must match exactly, including `apipay-verification=`. |
| No **Verify** option | It only appears while the domain is **Pending**. |
| Domain became inactive | It was not verified within 24 hours. Delete it and add it again. |
