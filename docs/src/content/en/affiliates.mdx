# Affiliate Program

The affiliate program helps you share ApiPay with customers, partners, or your community. When someone signs up through your referral link and completes their first successful plan payment, commission is credited to your affiliate balance.

This guide focuses on everyday use: finding your referral link, tracking results, and knowing when your balance is ready to withdraw.

---

## How it works

1. Open your **referral link** in the dashboard.
2. Share the link with someone who may want to use ApiPay.
3. The referred customer signs up and completes their first plan payment.
4. Commission is credited to your affiliate balance.

You can track clicks, successful referrals, available balance, withdrawn amount, and commission history from the dashboard.

---

## Open your affiliate page

1. Sign in to the [ApiPay Dashboard](https://my.apipay.vn).
2. Open **Affiliates** from the sidebar.
3. Review your referral link, referral code, and current stats.

If you do not have any referrals yet, the commission history table will be empty. You can start by copying your link and sharing it with relevant customers or partners.

---

## Share your referral link

On the **Affiliates** page, click **Copy** to copy your referral link.

You can share it through:

- Your website or landing page
- Email introductions
- Community posts
- Zalo, Facebook, Telegram, or customer support messages

Share the link in a clear context, for example when introducing a way to accept bank transfer payments in Vietnam.

---

## Change your referral code

You can customize your referral code to make the link easier to remember.

1. Click **Edit code**.
2. Enter a new code with at least 3 characters.
3. Click **Save**.
4. Copy the updated link before sharing it.

Choose a short, readable code tied to your brand or name. Avoid sensitive information such as phone numbers, identity numbers, or internal business data.

---

## Track commissions

The affiliate page shows the key numbers:

| Metric | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| Clicks | Number of times your referral link was opened |
| Successful referrals | Customers credited after their first plan payment |
| Available balance | Commission currently available to request for withdrawal |
| Withdrawn | Total commission already marked as withdrawn |

The **Commission history** section shows the invoice, referred customer, plan, and commission amount.

---

## Commission rules

- Current commission is **10% of the invoice value** for the referred customer's first successful plan payment.
- Available balance is shown directly in the dashboard.
- The current minimum withdrawal amount is **500,000 VND**.

---

## Tips

- Share ApiPay with people who actually need bank transfer payments in Vietnam.
- Use a memorable referral code so customers recognize you.
- Check the dashboard regularly to follow new referrals.
- Contact the ApiPay team if you need reconciliation or withdrawal support.
