# Affiliates

Share your ApiPay referral link. When someone you referred pays for their first plan, you earn a commission.

---

## How it works

1. Open **Affiliates** in the sidebar at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn) and copy your **Referral link**.
2. Send the link to people who want to use ApiPay, for example on your website, by email, Zalo or Facebook.
3. They sign up and pay for their first plan.
4. You get **10% of that invoice** in your **Available balance**.

---

## Change your referral code

The referral code is the last part of your link. To switch to an easier code:

1. Click **Edit code**.
2. Enter a new code (at least 3 characters) and save.
3. Copy the new link again before sharing.

---

## Track commissions

| Metric | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| Clicks | How many times your referral link was opened |
| Successful referrals | People who paid for their first plan |
| Available balance | Commission you can withdraw |
| Withdrawn | Total commission withdrawn |

**Commission history** lists each commission: referred user, date credited, plan, base amount and commission.

---

## Withdraw commission

1. Wait until your **Available balance** reaches at least 500,000 VND.
2. Open **Contact** in the sidebar and send ApiPay a withdrawal request.
