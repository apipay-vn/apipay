# How to Connect Banks

Connect a bank account so ApiPay can detect incoming money and notify you as soon as a customer pays. Want your own customers to link their banks? See [ApiPay Connect](/en/connect).

## Before you start

- An ApiPay account with an active plan (free or paid).
- The phone number registered with your bank.
- Documents by account type:
  - **Personal**: your 12-digit CCCD (citizen ID) number.
  - **Business** and **household business**: the tax code. Household businesses at MB Bank and VPBank enter the owner's CCCD instead.
  - **ACB**: no CCCD or tax code. Business and household business accounts also need an ACB Onebiz username.

---

## Supported banks

- **Auto**: confirm with the OTP your bank sends to your phone, ready to use right away.
- **Confirm in app**: you approve the link in your bank's app.
- **Activation request**: you submit your details and ApiPay contacts you to activate, usually within 24 hours.

For some banks, ApiPay issues you a virtual account (VA) number after you connect. The connect form tells you in advance if your bank uses one. Customers must then pay into that virtual account number.

## Steps

1. Sign in at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn), open **Banks** in the sidebar and click **Add Bank**.
2. Pick your bank and click **Connect**.
3. Choose the account type: **Personal**, **Business** or **Household business**. Each one is labeled **Auto** or **Needs approval**.
4. Fill in:
   - Account holder name (or company name), uppercase without accents
   - Account number
   - Phone number registered with the bank
   - CCCD, tax code or ACB Onebiz username, depending on bank and account type
   - Nickname (optional, only you see it in the dashboard)
5. Tick **I have read and agree to the terms above**, then click **Confirm** (or **Submit request** if the account needs activation).
6. Finish as shown on screen:
   - **Auto**: enter the OTP your bank sends to your phone.
   - **Confirm in app**: open your bank's app to approve. For Vietcombank you can scan the QR code shown on screen.
   - **Activation request**: no OTP. The bank stays **Pending** until ApiPay activates it.

Once connected, the account appears under **Banks** as **Active** and you can start creating payment requests. To rename, pause or remove a bank, see [Bank Management](/en/banking).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| No OTP received | Check that the phone number matches the one registered with your bank, wait a moment and try again. |
| CCCD rejected | Enter exactly 12 digits, with no spaces or special characters. |
| VIB says the payment speaker is not registered | Open the VIB Business app, choose **Nhận loa**, then come back and link again. |
| Connected but the bank is not active | Don't add the bank again. Send a ticket from **Contact → Tickets**. |
| Account number already linked | Each bank account can only be linked once. It may already belong to another ApiPay account. |
