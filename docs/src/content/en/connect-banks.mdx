# How to Connect Banks

Connect a Vietnamese bank account to your ApiPay dashboard to start receiving real-time bank transfer payments. Once linked, ApiPay automatically syncs incoming transactions and delivers payment notifications via webhook.

## Overview

Linking a bank account on ApiPay is a 2-step process:

1. **Select your bank** and account type (Personal or Business)
2. **Enter your account details** and verify ownership via OTP

Once linked, your bank account appears in the **Banks** section of the dashboard with a status of **Active**. You can then create payment requests and receive transfers within 1-2 seconds.

**Supported banks:**

| Bank                 | Personal | Business | Household | Features                 |
| -------------------- | :------: | :------: | :-------: | ------------------------ |
| **ACB**              |   Yes    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Bank account |
| **MB Bank**          |   Yes    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Virtual Account (VA) |
| **BIDV**             |   Yes    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Virtual Account (VA) |
| **OCB**              |   Yes    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Virtual Account (VA) |
| **VietinBank**      |    No    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Bank account |
| **VIB**              |   Yes    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Virtual Account (VA) |
| **Sacombank**        |   Yes    |    No    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Virtual Account (VA) |
| **VPBank**           |   Yes    |    No    |    No     | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Virtual Account (VA) |
| **PGBank**           |   Yes    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Bank account |
| **Co-opBank**        |   Yes    |   Yes    |    Yes    | OpenBanking, Sync inward, Virtual Account (VA) |

## Prerequisites

Before linking a bank account, make sure you have:

- An ApiPay account
- An active Vietnamese bank account from the supported list above
- The phone number registered with your bank (for OTP verification)
- For **MB Bank**, **VIB**, **Sacombank**, **VPBank**, **PGBank** Personal: your CCCD/ID card number (12 digits)
- For Business or Household Business accounts: your company/household Tax Code (MST)
- For **ACB Business**: an ACB OneBiz username
- **MB Bank**, **OCB**, **VIB** Business or Household, and **BIDV**/**VietinBank** Business: manual activation
- **PGBank**, **Co-opBank**: fully online, no manual activation

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Select Your Bank

1. Log in to the [ApiPay Dashboard](https://dashboard.apipay.vn)
2. Navigate to **Banks** in the sidebar
3. Click the **Add Bank** button in the top-right corner
4. On the **Link Bank Account** page, you'll see a list of supported banks
5. Find your bank in the table:
   - Check the **Recommended** badge for the preferred options
   - Look at **Account Types** — some banks support Personal, Business, or both
   - Note the **Features** — each bank supports different capabilities
6. Click **Connect** on your chosen bank

### Step 2: Choose Account Type

Most banks support three account types. Pick the one that matches your account:

- **Personal** — for individual bank accounts
- **Business** — for company bank accounts
- **Household Business** — for household business (hộ kinh doanh) accounts

Per-bank availability:

- **ACB**, **MB Bank**, **BIDV**, **OCB**, **VIB**, **PGBank**, **Co-opBank** — Personal, Business, Household Business
- **VietinBank** — Business, Household Business (Personal not supported)
- **VPBank** — Personal (Business, Household Business not supported)
- **Sacombank** — Personal, Household Business (Business not supported)

> **Manual activation.** MB/OCB/VIB Business or Household, and BIDV/VietinBank Business, use activation requests. You'll see **Activation Requested** until they complete. PGBank and Co-opBank are fully online.

Click the card that matches your account type to continue.

### Step 3: Enter Account Information

Fill in the required fields. The fields shown depend on which bank you selected.

#### Fields for All Banks

| Field              | Description                                                          |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Account Name**   | Your account holder name, written in CAPITAL LETTERS without accents |
| **Account Number** | Your bank account number                                             |
| **Phone Number**   | The phone number registered with your bank                           |

#### ACB — Personal Account

No additional fields required beyond the base fields above.

#### ACB — Business Account

| Additional Field        | Description                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **ACB OneBiz Username** | Your ACB OneBiz login username |

#### MB Bank — Personal Account

| Additional Field          | Description                                             |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CCCD / ID Card Number** | Your 12-digit CCCD number (Citizen Identification Card) |

#### MB Bank — Business Account

| Additional Field   | Description                              |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Tax Code (MST)** | Your company's Tax Identification Number |

#### BIDV — Personal Account

| Additional Field          | Description                             |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **CCCD / ID Card Number** | Your 12-digit CCCD number               |
| **Account Email**         | Your email address registered with BIDV |

#### OCB — Personal Account

No additional fields required beyond the base fields above.

#### VIB — Personal Account

| Additional Field          | Description               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **CCCD / ID Card Number** | Your 12-digit CCCD number |

#### Sacombank (STB) — Personal Account

| Additional Field          | Description               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **CCCD / ID Card Number** | Your 12-digit CCCD number |

#### Sacombank (STB) — Household Business Account

| Additional Field   | Description                                         |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tax Code (MST)** | Tax Identification Number of the household business |

> **Note for Sacombank:** Company (Business) accounts are not currently supported. Use Personal or Household Business.

#### PGBank — Personal Account

| Additional Field          | Description               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **CCCD / ID Card Number** | Your 12-digit CCCD number |

#### PGBank — Business / Household Business Account

| Additional Field   | Description                                                    |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tax Code (MST)** | Tax Identification Number of the company or household business |

> **Note:** All PGBank account types (Personal, Business, Household Business) are processed fully online with no manual activation required.

#### MB Bank / OCB / VIB — Business or Household Business

| Additional Field   | Description                                                    |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tax Code (MST)** | Tax Identification Number of the company or household business |

> **Manual activation:** Business and Household Business connections for MB Bank, OCB, and VIB are submitted for manual activation. ApiPay enables the account after the API contract is signed.

#### BIDV / VietinBank — Business Account

| Additional Field   | Description                              |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Tax Code (MST)** | Tax Identification Number of the company |

> **Manual activation:** BIDV and VietinBank Business connections are submitted for manual activation. ApiPay enables the account after the API contract is signed.

After filling in all fields:

1. Read the consent text about privacy policy and terms of use
2. Check the **I agree** checkbox
3. Click **Confirm** to proceed

A confirmation modal will appear — review the details and click **Confirm** again.

### Step 4: Verify with OTP (Online Flow)

For online connections, an **OTP (One-Time Password)** is sent automatically to the phone number registered with your bank.

1. Enter the 6-digit OTP code in the input field
2. Click **Confirm**
3. Once verified, your bank account is successfully linked

If the OTP code is incorrect, you'll see an error message. Re-enter the correct code and try again.

> **Manual activation accounts skip OTP.** Manual flows show **Activation Requested** instead of OTP.

## After Linking

Once your bank is linked successfully:

- The bank account appears in your **Banks** list with an **Active** status
- You can view transaction history filtered by that bank
- You can toggle the bank on/off using the status toggle
- ApiPay automatically registers a webhook to receive real-time transaction notifications

## Managing Your Connected Banks

### View Bank Details

1. Go to **Banks** in the sidebar
2. Click on any bank row to see full details:
   - Account holder name
   - Account number (partially masked)
   - Bank name and logo
   - Status (Active / Inactive)
   - Transaction history

### Toggle Bank Status

If you want to temporarily stop receiving payments on a specific bank:

1. Go to **Banks**
2. Find the bank you want to toggle
3. Click the **toggle switch** in the Actions column
4. The status changes between **Active** and **Inactive**

### Delete a Connected Bank

1. Go to **Banks**
2. Click the **...** (more) menu on the right side of the bank row
3. Select **Delete Bank**
4. Confirm with OTP (sent to your registered phone)
5. The bank is removed from your account

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                             | Solution                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| OTP not received                  | Make sure the phone number you entered matches the number registered with your bank. Wait a moment and try again.                                                                     |
| OTP code incorrect                | Double-check that you're entering the code sent to your phone. Codes are 6 digits.                                                                                                    |
| CCCD rejected                     | Make sure you enter exactly 12 digits without spaces or special characters.                                                                                                           |
| Bank shows "Pending" status       | After linking, the bank should become Active within seconds. If it stays Pending, try refreshing the page.                                                                            |
| Bank shows "Activation Requested" | This is expected for manual-activation flows (MB/OCB/VIB Business or Household, BIDV/VietinBank Business). ApiPay will contact you to sign the API contract and activate the account. |
| Account number already linked     | Each bank account can only be linked once. If you see this error, the account may already be connected to another ApiPay account.                                                     |
| Account type not available        | Sacombank does not currently support Business accounts — use Personal or Household Business instead.                                                                                  |
