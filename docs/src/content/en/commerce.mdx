# Commerce

Commerce lets you sell with a reusable payment link. Create a product and price once, then send the same link to every customer. Each customer who opens it enters their details, scans the QR code and gets a receipt by email.

Open **Integrations → Web App → Commerce** (Beta) at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn/en/integrations/commerce).

## How it works

1. **Create a product**: what you sell, for example "Pro plan".
2. **Add a price**: an amount in VND. A product can have several prices, for example monthly and yearly.
3. **Copy the payment link** and send it to customers. One link works for many people.
4. **The customer pays**: they enter their name and email, then scan the QR code. ApiPay creates a unique transfer content for each customer.
5. **You get notified** like any other payment. The customer gets a receipt by email.

Already know the buyer? On the product page, choose **Create one-time payment**.

## Default price and amounts

- Each product has a **default price**, used when you click **Copy payment link**. The first price becomes the default; use **Make default** to pick another.
- A price's amount cannot be edited. To change it, create a new price and **Archive** the old one. Archiving a price is also how you pause its link.
- A payment request keeps the amount it was created with. A reusable link always charges new customers the current amount.
- The internal name and lookup key are visible only to you, never to customers.

## Product images

Upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP image (up to 3 MB) when creating the product or on its detail page. The image shows on the payment page.

## Receipts

When a customer pays, ApiPay creates a receipt automatically. Find it in the **Invoices** tab.

- The receipt shows your details (seller) and the buyer's: name, email, plus tax code and address if they entered them. You can edit the buyer details later.
- Click **Download PDF** to save it. The customer also gets the PDF by email.
- A receipt confirms the customer paid. It is not an official VAT invoice.

## Customers

The **Customers** tab saves buyers automatically when they pay. Customers are identified by email. You can also add, edit or delete customers here.

**Send invoice** emails the customer a payment link and QR code for the price you choose. The receipt is created only after the customer pays.

## Not in Commerce

- Shopping cart or buying several products at once
- Automatic recurring charges from the customer's account
- Official VAT e-invoices

## Try it

1. Create a product, adding a price and image in the same form.
2. Click **Copy payment link**, open it, enter a name and email, then pay.
3. Find the receipt in the **Invoices** tab.

Want to test without moving real money? Turn on [Sandbox](/en/sandbox), create a one-time payment from a price, then simulate the payment.

Integrating through the API? See the [Commerce API](/en/api/commerce) or try it in the [Interactive Reference](/en/api-reference).
