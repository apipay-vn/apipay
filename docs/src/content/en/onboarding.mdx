# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to ApiPay! This guide will walk you through the complete process of setting up your account and start accepting payments.

## Why ApiPay?

ApiPay is a payment gateway that enables Vietnamese businesses to accept bank transfers through a simple API integration. No complex contracts, no hidden fees - just straightforward bank payment processing.

## The Merchant Journey

Here's what you'll accomplish:

1. **Sign Up** - Create your merchant account
2. **Choose a Plan** - Start with FREE TRIAL 72 Hours or pick a paid plan
3. **Connect Banks** - Link your Vietnamese bank accounts
4. **Configure Settings** - Set up webhooks and API keys
5. **Start Accepting Payments** - Create your first payment request

---

## Step 1: Sign Up

Visit [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn) and click **Sign Up**.

You can register using:

- **Email & Password** - Traditional registration
- **Magic Link** - Passwordless login via email

### Best Practices for Registration

- Use a **business email** - This establishes trust with your customers
- Choose a **strong password** or enable 2FA after signup
- Fill in your **business information** early - This appears on the payment page your customers see

## Step 3: Connect Your Bank Accounts

After subscribing, you'll connect your Vietnamese bank account(s). This is essential for receiving payments.

### Supported Banks

ApiPay supports the following major Vietnamese banks:

- ACB
- MB Bank
- BIDV
- OCB
- VietinBank
- VIB
- Sacombank (STB)
- VPBank (VPB)
- PGBank (PGB)
- Shinhan Bank (SHBVN)
- Co-opBank (COOPBANK)
- Vietcombank (VCB)

### Connection Process

1. Go to **Settings → Bank Accounts**
2. Click **Add Bank Account**
3. Select your bank from the list
4. Enter your account number
5. Verify with OTP sent to your registered phone
6. Account becomes active after verification

> **Important:** The bank account must be in your business name (or a legitimate representative name).

<Callout type="tip">
**Free Instant Feature:** Once your bank account is active, you can immediately use **[Payment Speaker](/en/payment-speaker)** to turn any phone or tablet at your counter into a real-time voice alert speaker without buying separate hardware.
</Callout>

## Step 4: Configure Your Integration

### Get API Keys

1. Navigate to **Integrations → API Keys**
2. Click **Create New Key**
3. Name your key (e.g., "Production", "Development")
4. Copy and store your keys securely

You'll receive:

- `accessKey` - Public identifier
- `secretKey` - Keep this private!

When calling the API, use `Authorization: Bearer <Auth>`, where `<Auth>` is `base64(accessKey:secretKey)`. See [Authentication](/en/api/authentication) for details.

### Set Up Webhooks (Optional but Recommended)

Webhooks notify your system when payments occur:

1. Go to **Settings → Webhooks**
2. Click **Add Webhook**
3. Enter your endpoint URL
4. Save and test with the test button

## Step 5: Create Your First Payment

### Via Dashboard

1. Go to **Payment Requests**
2. Click **Create Payment Request**
3. Select a connected bank account
4. Enter the amount
5. Add payment content/reference
6. Click **Create**

You'll receive a payment link to share with your customer.

### Via API

```javascript
const response = await fetch('https://app.apipay.vn/v1/client/payment-requests', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: {
    Authorization: 'Bearer <Auth>',
    'Content-Type': 'application/json',
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    bankPublicId: 'your-bank-id',
    amount: '100000',
    content: 'ORDER-12345',
  }),
});

const {data: {payUrl, qrUrl}} = await response.json();
// Share payUrl or qrUrl with your customer
```

## Best Practices

### For a Smooth Start

1. **Test with small amounts** - Create a few test payments of 1,000 VND to verify your setup works
2. **Turn on Payment Speaker at checkout** - Open the speaker URL on a counter phone or tablet to hear payments immediately
3. **Configure webhook first** - This ensures you never miss a payment notification
4. **Set up redirect URL** - Automatically redirect customers back to your site after payment
5. **Customize payment page** - Add your business name and logo for a professional experience

### For Long-Term Success

1. **Use multiple bank accounts** - Distribute volume across banks reduces congestion
2. **Monitor transaction analytics** - Use the dashboard to track payment patterns
3. **Enable 2FA** - Protect your account with two-factor authentication
4. **Keep API keys secure** - Rotate keys periodically and never expose them in client-side code
