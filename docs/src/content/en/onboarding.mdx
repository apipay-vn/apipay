# Getting Started

ApiPay lets you receive bank transfers and know the moment money arrives. You connect a bank, create payment requests and get notified by webhook. Transparent pricing, see [apipay.vn/pricing](https://apipay.vn/pricing).

Five steps to your first payment:

1. Create an account
2. Activate a plan
3. Connect a bank
4. Create an API key and webhook
5. Create your first payment request

---

## Step 1: Create an account

Go to [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn) and click **Sign Up**. Sign up with email and password, or with your Google or GitHub account.

Once you are in, turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) or add a [passkey](/en/passkeys) to protect your account.

---

## Step 2: Activate a plan

Open **Pricing** in the sidebar, fill in your phone number and address if asked, then click **Activate free**. The free plan needs no payment. You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time.

Plan details and how charges work: [Plans and Billing](/en/subscription).

---

## Step 3: Connect a bank

1. Open **Banks** in the sidebar and click **Add Bank**.
2. Pick your bank and account type (personal, business or household business) and fill in your details.
3. Confirm with an OTP or in your bank's app. Some account types need an activation request.

Supported banks and the full walkthrough: [Connect Banks](/en/connect-banks).

Once your bank is active, you can turn on [Payment Speaker](/en/payment-speaker) to hear incoming payments at the counter.

---

## Step 4: Create an API key and webhook

### API key

1. Open **Integrations → Web App → API Keys**.
2. Click **Generate API Key** and name it.
3. Store the `accessKey` and `secretKey` somewhere safe. Never put the `secretKey` in browser or app code.

How to use the key when calling the API: [API Authentication](/en/api/authentication).

### Webhook

1. Open **Webhooks** in the sidebar and click **Create Webhook**.
2. Enter your endpoint URL and save.

A new webhook receives notifications for all your banks. You can limit it to specific banks when you create it. Details: [Webhooks](/en/api/webhooks).

---

## Step 5: Create your first payment request

1. Open **Payment Requests** and click **Create Link**.
2. Pick a bank, enter the amount and transfer content.
3. Copy the payment link and send it to your customer.

Through the API:

```javascript
const response = await fetch('https://app.apipay.vn/v1/client/payment-requests', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: {
    Authorization: 'Bearer <Auth>',
    'Content-Type': 'application/json',
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    bankPublicId: 'your-bank-id',
    amount: '100000',
    content: 'ORDER-12345',
  }),
});

const {
  data: {payUrl, qrUrl},
} = await response.json();
// Send payUrl or qrUrl to your customer
```

Want to test the whole flow without moving real money? Use [Sandbox](/en/sandbox).
