# Subscription & Billing

Learn how to manage your ApiPay subscription, understand pricing, and optimize your plan choice.

---

| STARTER   | 250,000 VND/month   | 3             | Growing businesses     |
| PRO       | 400,000 VND/month   | 5             | Established businesses |
| TEAM      | 700,000 VND/month   | 10            | High-volume merchants  |
| COMPANY   | 1,700,000 VND/month | 25            | Large organizations    |

## Choosing the Right Plan

### Factors to Consider

1. **Transaction Volume** - More bank accounts means more capacity
2. **Business Growth** - Choose a plan that accommodates growth
3. **Peak Hours** - Multiple banks help during high-traffic periods

### Recommendations by Business Type

| Business Type     | Recommended Plan | Reason                    |
| ----------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------- |
| Solo entrepreneur | BASIC            | Start simple              |
| Small e-commerce  | STARTER          | Handle moderate volume    |
| Growing SaaS      | PRO              | Balance cost and capacity |
| Large marketplace | TEAM+            | High volume needs         |

> **Pro Tip:** You can always upgrade anytime. Starting with a lower plan and upgrading as you grow is perfectly fine.

## Purchasing a Plan

### Via Dashboard

1. Log in to [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn)
2. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
3. Browse available plans
4. Click **Select Plan** on your choice
5. Complete payment

### Payment Methods

Currently, you can purchase plans via:

- **Bank transfer** - Use the generated invoice to pay via your connected bank

After payment is confirmed, your plan activates immediately.

## Managing Your Subscription

### Viewing Current Plan

1. Navigate to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Your current plan details are displayed at the top:
   - Plan name
   - Price
   - Bank account limit
   - Renewal date

### Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Select your desired plan
3. Complete payment
4. Changes take effect immediately
5. You'll only be charged the prorated difference (contact us for more detail)

### Downgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Select a lower plan
3. Confirm the change
4. **Downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle**
5. Your current bank accounts above the new limit will need to be removed

> **Warning:** Downgrading may result in losing access to features. Make sure to adjust your bank accounts accordingly.

---

## Billing Cycle

- **Monthly billing** - Charged on the same day each month
- **Proration** - Upgrades are charged prorated to your current cycle
- **Invoices** - All invoices are available in **Settings → Invoices**
- **No refunds** - No refunds are provided for used or partial billing periods

### Renewal Process

1. 3 days before renewal, you'll receive an email reminder
2. Auto-renewal happens on the renewal date
3. If payment fails, you'll be notified immediately
4. After 7 days of failed payment, access may be restricted

---

## Tips for Managing Costs

### Optimize Your Plan

1. **Start with BASIC** - Start lean before scaling up
2. **Track usage** - Monitor your bank account usage in the dashboard
3. **Upgrade strategically** - Upgrade only when you hit limits

### Avoid Unexpected Charges

1. **Set budget alerts** - Monitor your transaction patterns
2. **Remove unused banks** - If a bank account is inactive, consider removing it
3. **Review invoices** - Check monthly invoices for accuracy
