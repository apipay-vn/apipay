# Subscription & Billing

Learn how to manage your ApiPay subscription and pricing.

---

## Plan Overview

ApiPay uses a **Usage Quota Billing** model. The free plan includes 50 successful transactions/month and 1 bank account. Paid tiers range from 200 to 500,000 transactions/month and support multi-bank connections simultaneously. Choose a **billing term** of 1, 3, 6 (5% discount), or 12 months (10% discount) at checkout. Listed prices are final checkout totals with no extra taxes added.

### Term Discounts

| Term      | Discount | Example (QUOTA 1200 — 348,000 VND/month) |
| --------- | -------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1 month   | 0%       | 348,000 VND                              |
| 3 months  | 0%       | 1,044,000 VND (348,000 VND/month)        |
| 6 months  | 5%       | 1,983,600 VND (330,600 VND/month)        |
| 12 months | 10%      | 3,758,400 VND (313,200 VND/month)        |

The **effective monthly price** is lower for discounted 6-month and 12-month terms. You'll see the subtotal, discount, and effective monthly price before confirming payment.

### Balance Overage & Hold Cap

- At 80% and 100% of included monthly quota, ApiPay sends usage notices so you can top up or upgrade before extra usage starts.
- After 100%, successful extra transactions are deducted from your prepaid balance at your plan’s rate. The free plan uses 350 VND/extra transaction.
- If the prepaid balance cannot cover an extra transaction, ApiPay places the account on hold and still allows up to 60 additional successful transactions.
- When the hold reaches 60 transactions, connected banks can be removed until you add enough balance. Failed transactions are never charged.

## Choosing the Right Plan

### Factors to Consider

1. **Average Monthly Transaction Volume** - Use the free plan to validate a small setup, then pick a paid plan that matches or slightly exceeds your typical volume to unlock lower effective unit costs (down to 150 VND/tx).
2. **Seasonal Peaks** - Keep enough prepaid balance for spikes; extra successful transactions are deducted at your plan’s rate.
3. **Multi-Bank Architecture** - Paid quota plans support multi-bank connections to power multi-channel sales and reconciliation.

### Recommendations by Scale

| Scale / Business Type  | Recommended Plan | Included Tx/mo |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| Trial / first setup    | FREE             | 50 tx          |

| Enterprise (> 500k tx) | Enterprise       | Contact Sales  |

## Purchasing a Plan

### Via Dashboard

1. Log in to [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn)
2. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
3. Browse available plans
4. Click **Select Plan** on your choice
5. Complete payment

### Payment Methods

Currently, you can purchase plans via:

- **Bank transfer** - Use the generated payment request and invoice to transfer to ApiPay

After payment is confirmed, your plan activates immediately.

## Managing Your Subscription

### Viewing Current Plan

1. Navigate to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Your current plan details are displayed at the top:
   - Plan name
   - Price
   - Bank account limit
   - Renewal date

### Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Select your desired plan
3. Complete payment
4. Changes take effect immediately
5. The selected term becomes your default renewal term

### Downgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings → Subscription**
2. Select a lower plan
3. Confirm the change
4. **Downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle**
5. Your current bank accounts above the new limit will need to be removed

> **Warning:** Downgrading may result in losing access to features. Make sure to adjust your bank accounts accordingly.

## Billing Cycle

- **Flexible terms** — Choose 1, 3, 6, or 12 months at checkout
- **Term discounts** — 6 months saves 5%, and 12 months saves 10%
- **Effective monthly price** — Shown at checkout so you can compare the real cost per month
- **Invoices** — All invoices are available in **Settings → Invoices**, each showing the billing term
- **No refunds** — No refunds are provided for used or partial billing periods

### Renewal Process

1. ApiPay creates a renewal invoice 5 days before expiry using your last paid term (so you keep your discount)
2. You can pay the draft invoice or switch to another term before payment
3. Your plan extends after ApiPay confirms the bank transfer
4. After the grace period, unpaid renewal invoices are cancelled and access may be restricted

## Tips for Managing Costs

### Optimize Your Plan

1. **Start free** - Validate your bank connection and integration before scaling up
2. **Track usage** - Monitor transaction usage and prepaid balance in the dashboard
3. **Upgrade strategically** - Upgrade before predictable volume exceeds the free or current paid quota

### Avoid Unexpected Charges

1. **Keep prepaid balance funded** - Extra usage is deducted from balance as it happens
2. **Remove unused banks** - If a bank account is inactive, consider removing it
3. **Review invoices and balance history** - Check monthly invoices and credit-balance entries for accuracy
