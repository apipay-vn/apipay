# Plans & billing

Choose a plan, pay and renew under **Pricing** in the sidebar at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn).

---

## Plans

ApiPay charges by the number of successful transactions per month. The **free plan** includes 60 transactions per month; paid plans range from 200 to 500,000. Every plan, including free, can connect multiple banks and has every feature: webhooks, VietQR, Commerce, Payment Speaker, custom domains.

Listed prices are the amount you pay, VAT included. See every plan at [apipay.vn/pricing](https://apipay.vn/pricing).

### Term discounts

You pick a term at checkout. Before you confirm, you see the total, the discount and the monthly equivalent.

| Term | Discount | Example (1,200-transaction plan, 348,000 VND/month) |
|------|----------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| 1 month | 0% | 348,000 VND |
| 3 months | 0% | 1,044,000 VND |
| 6 months | 5% | 1,983,600 VND (330,600 VND/month) |
| 12 months | 10% | 3,758,400 VND (313,200 VND/month) |

### Going over your quota

- ApiPay alerts you at 80% and 100% of your monthly quota.
- Transactions over the quota are charged to your **prepaid balance** at your plan's unit price. The free plan charges 380 VND per extra transaction.
- Keep enough balance. When it runs out, you need to top up to keep using the service.
- Failed transactions are never charged.

## Activate the free plan

1. Sign in at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn) and fill in your phone number and address if asked.
2. Open **Pricing**.
3. Click **Activate free**.

The plan starts right away and never expires. Each phone number gets one free plan.

## Buy or upgrade a paid plan

1. Open **Pricing** and choose a quota plan.
2. Choose a term.
3. Pay with your **balance** or by **bank transfer** using the QR code. The plan activates as soon as ApiPay receives the money.

When you upgrade mid-term, choose one of two options:

- **Continue the current cycle** (default): keep the quota you have used and pay only the difference.
- **Start a fresh cycle**: pay the full price of the new plan and the quota resets to 0. The unused value of your old plan is added to your balance.

During a running term you can only move to a higher plan. To downgrade, wait until the term ends.

## Top up your balance

In the **Balance** card, click **Top up**, choose an amount and pay by QR transfer. The balance updates automatically. Click **View balance history** to see every credit and debit.

---

## Renewal

- 3 days before your plan expires, ApiPay issues a renewal invoice for your last term, keeping its discount. You can change the term before paying.
- Turn on **Auto-renew from balance** to have ApiPay pay renewal invoices from your balance. The balance must cover the whole invoice.
- After the expiry date you get a 3-day grace period to pay. After that, the unpaid invoice is cancelled and the service is restricted.

All invoices are under **Invoices** in the sidebar.

## Cancel your plan

In **Pricing**, open **Cancel subscription** and choose:

- **Cancel at period end**: keep using the plan until the paid term ends, then it does not renew.
- **Cancel now**: the plan ends immediately and unused value is not refunded.

Cancelling does not affect your connected banks.

## Refunds

ApiPay does not refund cash. Unused value goes to your balance only when you choose **Start a fresh cycle** while upgrading. Balance cannot be withdrawn as cash.
