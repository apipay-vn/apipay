# Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in to ApiPay without a password. You just confirm with Face ID, fingerprint, Windows Hello or your screen lock.

Your fingerprint or face is checked only on your device; ApiPay never receives it. A passkey is tied to the real ApiPay site, so it cannot be used on a fake website.

---

## Create a passkey

1. Sign in at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn).
2. Open **Settings → Account** and choose the **Credentials** tab.
3. Under **Passkeys**, click **Add passkey**.
4. Enter your current password or 2FA code when asked.
5. Confirm on your device. The passkey is saved automatically.

The passkey is named after your device and browser, for example "MacBook Pro Chrome Passkey". You can rename it later.

Each account has **one passkey**. To switch devices, delete the old passkey and create a new one.

> If the **Add passkey** button is greyed out, this browser or device does not support passkeys yet. Try another browser or device.

---

## Sign in with a passkey

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Continue with passkey**.
2. Confirm on your device.

You can still sign in with your password, Google or GitHub. Keep at least one of these for when you change or lose your device.

---

## Rename or delete a passkey

In **Settings → Account → Credentials**, click the rename or delete icon next to the passkey. ApiPay asks for your current password or 2FA code first.

---

## Supported devices

| Platform | Browsers | How you confirm |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| macOS | Chrome, Safari, Edge | Touch ID or your Mac password |
| Windows | Chrome, Edge | Windows Hello |
| iOS | Safari, Chrome | Face ID or Touch ID |
| Android | Chrome | Fingerprint or screen lock |

---

## Frequently asked questions

### What if I lose my device?

Sign in with your password, Google or GitHub, delete the old passkey, then create a new one on the device you use now.

### Do I still need 2FA with a passkey?

Keep 2FA on, especially if your account can still sign in with a password. Some sensitive actions ask you to confirm again with a passkey or 2FA.
