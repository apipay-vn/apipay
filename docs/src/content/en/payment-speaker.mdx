# Payment Speaker

Payment Speaker reads the amount aloud every time a customer's transfer lands in your account. No special device needed: just open a link on a phone, tablet or computer with a speaker at your counter. It is included in every plan at no extra cost.

Your cashier hears the announcement and knows the money has arrived, without holding the owner's phone or trusting a customer's screenshot.

---

## Setup

1. Sign in at [my.apipay.vn](https://my.apipay.vn) and open **Integrations → Payment Speaker**.
2. Under **Start New Session**, pick the bank account to get incoming-payment alerts for and choose a voice. Northern, Central and Southern accents are available, in male and female voices.
3. Click **Start Session**, then copy the **Session URL**.
4. Open that URL in a browser on the counter device.
5. Tap the screen once so the browser allows sound.

Under **Advanced settings** you can adjust speed, volume, the delay before speaking, and whether the bank name is read. Use **Try this voice** to preview.

Each bank runs at most one session. With several banks, start one session per bank. One session URL can be open on several devices at once.

The speaker screen has a button to show your QR code for customers to scan, and a list of recent transactions for quick checks.

> **Keep the link private.** Anyone with the session URL can see incoming payments to that account. If the link leaks, stop the session and start a new one.

---

## Tips

- **Use a dedicated device**: an old phone or tablet at the counter, plugged in, with the speaker page open.
- **Pair a Bluetooth speaker** if your shop is noisy.
- **Add to Home Screen** in Safari or Chrome to open the speaker page like an app.

---

## Managing sessions

The Payment Speaker page lists running sessions. Click **Stop Session** to end one, or **Stop All** to end every session on every device.

---

## Frequently asked questions

### Why do I have to tap the screen when I open the link?

Browsers block websites from playing sound before you interact with the page. Tap once when you open it and every announcement plays automatically after that.

### What device do I need?

Any device with a speaker, an internet connection and a web browser: phone, tablet, computer or Android POS terminal.
