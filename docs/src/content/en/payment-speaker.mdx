# Payment Speaker

**Payment Speaker** is a built-in, **100% free** feature in ApiPay that announces incoming bank transfers in real time using clear, natural voice audio.

Instead of spending millions of VND on dedicated hardware payment speakers, you can simply use an existing phone, tablet, or computer at the checkout counter and open the speaker session in any web browser.

---

## Why use Payment Speaker?

In busy retail, dining, and service environments, checking incoming bank transfers manually often causes friction:

- **Rush hour bottleneck:** Cashiers and baristas are busy preparing orders and cannot constantly check banking apps or wait for SMS messages on the owner's phone.
- **Counterfeit screenshot fraud:** Fraudsters can show fake transfer screenshots. Without real-time verification, stores risk product loss.
- **Account security & privacy:** You don't need to install banking apps or share sensitive banking credentials with checkout staff.

<Callout type="tip">
**The ApiPay solution:** When a transfer arrives, the speaker plays a friendly *"Ting"* chime followed by a clear voice announcement with the exact amount and receiving bank. Staff can hear the confirmation immediately and fulfill orders with confidence.
</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Setup

Setting up Payment Speaker takes less than a minute:

### Step 1: Open Payment Speaker in Dashboard

1. Sign in to the [ApiPay Dashboard](https://my.apipay.vn).
2. In the left sidebar, navigate to **Integrations** and select **Payment Speaker** (Loa thanh toán).

### Step 2: Select Bank Account & Voice

1. Under **Start new session**:
   - **Bank Account:** Select the active bank account you want to monitor.
   - **Voice:** Choose your preferred voice (e.g., *Ban Mai - Northern Female*, *Minh Quang - Southern Male*, *Gia Huy - Central Male*, etc.).
2. Click **Start session**.

<Callout type="info">
Each bank account runs an independent speaker session. If you manage multiple bank accounts for different checkout counters, you can start a separate session for each account.
</Callout>

### Step 3: Open Session URL on Counter Device

1. Once started, a unique **Session URL** is generated (e.g., `https://pay.apipay.vn/ting/abc123xyz`).
2. Click **Copy** to copy the URL or send it to the device at the checkout counter (phone, tablet, POS terminal, or PC).
3. Open the URL in the device's web browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.).
4. **Important:** Tap or click anywhere on the speaker page once to grant audio playback permission to the browser.

## Speaker Screen Experience

The speaker interface is clean, intuitive, and designed specifically for busy cashier desks:

1. **Live Connection Indicator:**
   - Visual pulsing waves animate when incoming payments arrive or when voice audio is playing.
   - Central **"Test Audio" / Replay** button: Tap anytime to test speaker volume or replay the last announcement.
2. **Receive QR Code Modal:**
   - Tap **View QR Code** in the top bar to display the bank QR code on screen.
   - Turn the tablet toward customers so they can scan and transfer instantly.
3. **Recent Transactions List (Last 10):**
   - Displays time, amount, and bank for recent transfers.
   - Staff can quickly verify past transactions without calling the manager.
4. **Customization & Display:**
   - Toggle between Light and Dark mode for optimal viewing day and night.
   - Switch between Vietnamese and English interface language.

## Operational Tips for Store Owners

### 1. Repurpose spare phones or desk tablets
Mount an older smartphone or tablet at the cashier counter, keep it plugged into power, and leave the Payment Speaker tab open.

### 2. Connect a small Bluetooth speaker
In noisy environments (coffee grinders, background music, busy retail stores), pair the device with a small Bluetooth speaker placed near the cashier station for crystal-clear sound.

### 3. Add to Home Screen (PWA experience)
In Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android), tap **Add to Home Screen**. The speaker URL will launch full-screen like a dedicated app with a single tap.

### 4. Safe staff delegation
The speaker URL only broadcasts audio and displays transaction history for the selected bank account. Counter staff cannot access the admin Dashboard or view sensitive financial settings.

- **Stop All Sessions:** Click **Stop all** to immediately close all active speaker sessions across all devices.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is there any recurring subscription fee for Payment Speaker?
No. Payment Speaker is completely free and included with your ApiPay account.

### Do I need to buy dedicated speaker hardware?
No. It runs entirely in your web browser on existing phones, tablets, laptops, or Android POS terminals.

### Why do I need to tap the screen once when first opening the link?
Modern web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge) require a single user gesture before allowing audio playback to prevent unsolicited background noise. After the first tap, all incoming transfer announcements will play automatically.

### Can I open the same speaker link on multiple devices?
Yes. You can open the same session URL on multiple devices across your store so staff in different areas can hear payments simultaneously.
