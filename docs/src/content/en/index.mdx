# Welcome to ApiPay

ApiPay lets you accept Vietnamese bank transfers through an API. You create a payment link, the customer scans the QR or transfers, and ApiPay records the transaction and sends a webhook to your system.

![ApiPay documentation overview](/apipay-banner.jpg)

## Key features

- **Payment links** with a QR code and transfer details
- **Automatic confirmation** when money arrives
- **Webhooks** that report payments to your system almost instantly
- **Free Payment Speaker**: voice alerts for incoming transfers at the counter, on any device
- **Many banks**: see the [supported banks](/en/connect-banks)
- **No percentage fees**: only successful transactions count toward your quota
- **Free plan** to start, upgrade by quota when you need to. See [Pricing](https://apipay.vn/pricing)

## How it works

1. **Create a payment link** with the API or the dashboard.
2. **Send the link to your customer**: they scan the QR or transfer.
3. **ApiPay records the transaction** and matches it to the payment request.
4. **You receive a webhook**, and the counter speaker announces the payment.
