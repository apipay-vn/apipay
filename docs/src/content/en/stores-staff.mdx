# Stores and Staff

When several people handle payments, you can group receiving accounts by store, invite staff and choose what each person can see or do. This feature is in **Beta**.

- A **store** is a group of receiving accounts, for example one branch or one brand.
- **Staff** sign in with their own email and password and only see the stores you assign to them.

Open **Integrations → Stores** in the sidebar. The page has four tabs: **Stores**, **Staff**, **Permissions** and **Activity**. Only the account owner can open it.

---

## Setup

1. **Create a store**: name it and add receiving accounts. Newly connected accounts stay outside any store until you add them.
2. **Add staff**: enter their email and set a starting password. ApiPay does not email passwords, so send it to them through a secure channel yourself.
3. **Choose access**: assign **Viewer**, **Cashier** or a custom permission set.
4. **Review activity** in the **Activity** tab.

---

## Stores

- Each receiving account belongs to at most one store. You can move an account to another store or remove it from a store at any time.
- Payments belong to a store through the account that received them. Move an account and its payments show up under the new store.
- A staff member can belong to several stores and switch between them with the store picker.

---

## Access

Each staff member gets one access level per assigned store.

| Level | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Viewer** | View transactions and payment requests. Cannot create, cancel or export. |
| **Cashier** | Everything Viewer can, plus create and cancel payment requests. Cannot export. |
| **Custom permission set** | Only the permissions you choose. |

### Custom permission sets

Create them in the **Permissions** tab and reuse them for many staff.

| Group | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| Payments | View transactions, Export transactions¹, View payment requests, Create payment requests¹, Cancel payment requests¹ |
| Sales receipts | View sales receipts |
| Bank accounts and webhooks | View bank accounts, View webhooks |

¹ Turning this on also turns on the matching view permission.

Three presets get you started: **Store manager** (all permissions), **Accountant** (view and export transactions; view payment requests, sales receipts and bank accounts), **Staff** (view transactions; view and create payment requests). Presets only pre-select permissions; click **Save** to apply. When you edit a permission set, the change applies right away to every staff member using it.

---

## What staff can never do

Whatever their permissions, staff cannot:

- Connect, pause or remove bank accounts (they can only view them).
- Create, edit or delete webhooks, or see signing secrets.
- View, create or revoke API keys.
- See your plan, billing or ApiPay invoices.
- Use Sandbox Mode or manage other staff.

Staff also cannot see data from stores they are not assigned to.

---

## Activity log

The **Activity** tab records staff sign-ins and changes to staff, permissions and receiving accounts. You can filter by event type.

---

## Good to know

- **Some sensitive actions need confirmation with a passkey or 2FA.** See [Passkeys](/en/passkeys).
- **Deleting a store or staff member revokes access immediately.** Move receiving accounts and staff to another store before deleting a store.
- **Never give API keys to staff.** An API key acts with full owner access and is not limited by store.
- **Staff can use the ApiPay mobile app** with the email and password you set, and only see what they have permission for.
