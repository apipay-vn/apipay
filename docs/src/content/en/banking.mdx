# Bank Account Management

Bank accounts are the foundation of receiving payments on ApiPay. Each connected bank account creates a payment channel that your customers can use to transfer money to you in real time.

---

## How It Works

When a customer makes a payment to your linked bank account, ApiPay:

1. **Detects** the incoming transfer via Pay2S OpenBanking
2. **Matches** the transfer to the corresponding payment request
3. **Notifies** you instantly via webhook

This means you don't need to manually reconcile payments — everything is automatic.

## Supported Banks

| Bank | Code | Personal | Business | Key Features |
|------|------|:--------:|:--------:|--------------|
| **ACB** | ACB | Yes | Yes | OpenBanking, Sync inward/outward |
| **MB Bank** | MBB | Yes | Yes | OpenBanking, Sync inward/outward |
| **BIDV** | BIDV | Yes | No | OpenBanking, Virtual Account |

Other banks (VietinBank, VietcomBank) are coming soon.

## Key Concepts

### Account Types

When connecting a bank, you'll choose between:

- **Personal** — for individual bank accounts
- **Business** — for company accounts (ACB and MB Bank only; BIDV is personal only)

### Statuses

After connecting, a bank account has one of these statuses:

- **Active** — fully operational, receiving payments
- **Pending** — OTP verified but final confirmation in progress
- **Inactive** — temporarily disabled; payments won't be routed here

### Virtual Accounts (BIDV)

BIDV supports **Virtual Account (VA)**. The system generates VAs based on a **Merchant ID** that you define. **Important:** Real-time notifications are only triggered when money is transferred directly to this Virtual Account.

## Managing Multiple Banks

### Why Connect More Than One?

- **Multiple payment options** — Different banks suit different customer preferences
- **Load distribution** — Spread transactions across accounts to avoid congestion
- **Redundancy** — Backup accounts ensure continuous service if one bank has issues
- **Transaction limits** — Some banks have daily transfer limits; additional accounts give you more capacity

### Recommended Setup

| Volume | Recommended |
|--------|-------------|
| Low | 1-2 banks |
| Medium | 3-5 banks |
| High | Use all available slots |

## Dashboard Navigation

In the ApiPay Dashboard, the **Banks** section is accessible from the sidebar:

- **Banks** (`/banks`) — View all connected accounts, add new ones, view transactions per bank
- Each bank row shows status, account type, and actions

> For step-by-step instructions on connecting a bank, see the [Connect Banks guide](/en/connect-banks).

## Security Best Practices

- **Use business accounts** — Keep payment accounts in your business name
- **Limit access** — Only authorized team members should manage connected banks
- **Monitor transactions** — Regularly review your transaction history for suspicious activity
- **Enable 2FA** — Add an extra layer of security to your dashboard account
