# Bank Account Management

Learn how to connect, manage, and optimize your Vietnamese bank accounts in ApiPay.

---

## Why Bank Accounts Matter

Bank accounts are the foundation of receiving payments. Each connected bank account creates a payment channel that customers can use to transfer money to you.

### Key Benefits

- **Multiple payment options** - Different banks for different customer preferences
- **Load distribution** - Spread transactions across accounts to avoid congestion
- **Redundancy** - Backup accounts ensure continuous service

## Connecting a Bank Account

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Navigate to Bank Accounts**
   - Go to **Settings → Bank Accounts** in your dashboard

2. **Click "Add Bank Account"**
   - You'll see a list of supported banks

3. **Select Your Bank**
   - Choose from the dropdown list
   - Each bank shows its logo and name

4. **Enter Account Information**
   - Enter your account number
   - Enter the account name (as registered with the bank)

5. **OTP Verification**
   - An OTP will be sent to your registered phone number with the bank
   - Enter the 6-digit code to verify ownership

6. **Confirmation**
   - Account status will be "PENDING" initially
   - After successful verification, it becomes "ACTIVE"

### What You'll Need

- A valid Vietnamese bank account
- Phone number registered with the bank (for OTP)
- Account must be in your business name or authorized representative name

## Managing Bank Accounts

### View All Accounts

1. Go to **Settings → Bank Accounts**
2. You'll see a table with:
   - Bank name and logo
   - Account number (masked)
   - Account name
   - Status (ACTIVE/PENDING/INACTIVE)
   - Actions

### Toggle Account Status

You can temporarily disable a bank account:

1. Click the toggle next to the account
2. Disabled accounts won't appear in new payment requests
3. Existing payment requests still work
4. Toggle back on anytime

### Delete a Bank Account

To remove a bank account:

1. Click **Delete** on the account row
2. Confirm your decision
3. An OTP will be sent for verification
4. Enter OTP to confirm deletion
5. Account is removed

> **Warning:** Deleting an account is permanent. Make sure you don't have any pending payments using that account.

## Best Practices

### Optimal Bank Setup

1. **Start with 1-2 banks** - Begin with your primary bank
2. **Add backup accounts** - Connect a second bank for redundancy
3. **Consider customer preferences** - MB and ACB are popular with Vietnamese consumers

### Managing Multiple Accounts

| Scenario      | Recommendation          |
| ------------- | ----------------------- |
| Low volume    | 1-2 accounts            |
| Medium volume | 3-5 accounts            |
| High volume   | Use all available slots |

### Account Health

1. **Monitor status** - Check dashboard regularly for any issues
2. **Keep accounts active** - Inactive accounts may be flagged
3. **Update promptly** - If bank details change, update immediately

## Troubleshooting

### OTP Not Received

- **Check phone number** - Ensure it's the number registered with the bank
- **Wait a moment** - SMS may be delayed
- **Check spam** - Some banks send from short codes
- **Try again** - Request a new OTP

### Account Stuck on Pending

- **Verify OTP** - Make sure you entered the correct code
- **Contact support** - If the issue persists, reach out for help

### Verification Failed

- **Check account details** - Ensure name matches exactly what's registered
- **Bank restrictions** - Some accounts may have transfer limits
- **Try another account** - Use a different account if issues persist

## Security Considerations

### Account Security

- **Keep accounts in business name** - Never use third-party accounts
- **Limit access** - Only authorized personnel should manage bank accounts
- **Enable 2FA** - Add an extra layer of security to your dashboard

### Monitoring

1. **Review transactions** - Check dashboard for any suspicious activity
2. **Set up alerts** - Configure webhook notifications for all events
3. **Audit regularly** - Periodically review connected accounts

## API Integration

You can manage bank accounts programmatically:

```javascript
// List connected bank accounts
const banks = await fetch("https://app.apipay.vn/v1/client/banks", {
	headers: {
		Authorization: "Bearer <Auth>",
	},
});

// Get bank summary
const summary = await fetch("https://app.apipay.vn/v1/client/banks/summary", {
	headers: {
		Authorization: "Bearer <Auth>",
	},
});
```
