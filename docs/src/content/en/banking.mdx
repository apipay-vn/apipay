# Bank Account Management

Your bank account is where customers send you money. Haven't connected one yet? See [Connect Banks](/en/connect-banks).

## How it works

When a customer transfers money into a connected account, ApiPay:

1. Records the incoming payment.
2. Matches it to the right payment request.
3. Notifies you by webhook, notification channel or Payment Speaker.

No manual reconciliation per order. Every plan, including the free plan, can connect multiple banks.

## Statuses

- **Active**: receiving payments normally.
- **Pending**: finishing the connection, usually within a few seconds. Accounts that need an activation request stay here until ApiPay activates them.
- **Inactive**: you paused the account and ApiPay does not receive payments through it.

## Common actions

Open **Banks** in the sidebar. Each row has a status switch and a **...** menu:

- **Turn on or off**: flip the switch to pause or resume payments.
- **View transactions**: transactions for this account only.
- **Edit nickname**: up to 50 characters, only you see it in the dashboard.
- **Delete Bank**: remove the account from ApiPay.
